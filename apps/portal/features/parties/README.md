# parties

The party family's forms and acts (SCR-PTY-01 to 05, 11 to 13): the server actions (`actions.ts`), the words and
formatting the screens share (`copy.ts`), the reading of a form (`fields.ts`), and the client components the acts
need - the identification and review decision forms, the grant, amendment and revocation forms, the side panel
forms of the party detail, the filters - each keeping only what the person is typing.
`PremisesTimeline.tsx` draws a premises' relationships and changes, with the console's relationship question, for
SCR-PTY-07 on the console and on the premises portal; `LimitationFacts.tsx` the register's limitation on the portal.
