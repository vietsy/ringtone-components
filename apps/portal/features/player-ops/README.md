# player-ops

The Player device on the consoles and in public (SCR-PLR-01 to 03, 07, 10, 11, 13, 15, SCR-IAM-07, 08): the server
actions (`actions.ts`), how a form is read (`fields.ts`), the words and shapes of the states (`copy.ts`), and the
small client components a link or a form cannot express: the condition and credential dialogs, the enrolment form,
the apply and assess dialogs and the filters that are addresses. The device's own screens (SCR-PLR-16 to 18) are the
Android app's (rightone-mobile), not the portal's.
