# [premises]

    page.tsx   SCR-PTY-07  one premises, as a premises actor reads it

GET /premises/{id} for the principal or a delegate: the entry with its own classification, address and area, the party
operating it now, its state and licensing status, what the reader may do there, its relationships with their periods
and end causes, the changes recorded against it (GET /premises/{id}/relationships, /changes), and the register's
limitation. A premises the reader's authority does not cover is NOT_YOUR_PREMISES and discloses nothing.
