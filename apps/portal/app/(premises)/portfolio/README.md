# portfolio

    page.tsx               SCR-PTY-19  the premises portfolio: the party the person acts for, its premises their authority covers
    [premises]/page.tsx    SCR-PTY-07  one premises of the portfolio on the premises portal: its facts, relationships and changes

The premises portal's Premises section, for the principal (ACT-PRE-PRI) and for a delegate (ACT-PRE-DEL) alike
(`requirePrincipal`, whose `actor` the party register decides). The principal sees every premises the party operates;
a delegate sees only those their authority names, each with what they may do there (DR-201, `authorityToActAt`). The
party the view is for is the heading; another party the person holds an authority for is switched to by choosing it
on RightOne ID and is never listed here (FR-PTY-007). A premises outside the reader's authority is NOT_YOUR_PREMISES.
The principal opens the party's authorities (SCR-PTY-11) from here; a delegate has no such control.
