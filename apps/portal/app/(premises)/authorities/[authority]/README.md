    page.tsx         SCR-PTY-13  one authority: its versions, the acts performed under it, and its revocation
    amend/page.tsx   SCR-PTY-12  a new version of it

A revocation takes effect from now or from a date and time and needs a ground; one backdated over an act performed
under the authority is refused naming the act, with Revoke from now offered as a control. Revoking leads back to the
list; a revocation sent again from a page opened before is ALREADY_REVOKED.
