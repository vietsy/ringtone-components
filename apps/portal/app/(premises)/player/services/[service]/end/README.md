The date the service ends (blank is the earliest CFG-291 allows, tomorrow in Vietnam) and an optional reason, kept in
the module's audit log. End the service asks first, naming the premises and the instant; recorded, SCR-VEN-04 shows
the ending waiting. An earlier date is refused beside the field; a service ended or with an ending waiting is
ALREADY_ENDED in the header, the form unavailable. Views: not-authorised, loading, error.
