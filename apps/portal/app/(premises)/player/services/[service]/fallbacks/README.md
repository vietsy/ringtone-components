    page.tsx    SCR-VEN-09  fallbacks

Every recorded period whose scheduled playlist could not play, what played instead and whether the notice went;
Open the playlist goes to SCR-VEN-07, unavailable for an archived playlist. Views: empty, not-authorised, loading,
error. The rows are the eligibility sweep's (`Venue.ReevaluateEligibility` in the mock backend), recorded from the
resolution itself: Pasteur's weekend schedule of Acoustic cuối tuần falls back from 25 September 2026, when its only
track stopped being eligible, and a playlist that loses its last eligible track in this world adds its own rows. A row
with no notice reference reads "Notice not sent" (EXC-VEN-06).
