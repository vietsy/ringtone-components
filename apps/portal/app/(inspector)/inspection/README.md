# inspection

The inspection console on the authority's desk (R1 ConsoleShell, at 1280 px). Its rail holds the lists and the
inspections and records of the authority (`features/inspection/ConsoleFrame.tsx`).

    lists/page.tsx                     SCR-VER-07  Inspection lists
    lists/[list]/page.tsx              SCR-VER-08  List supply and entries
    records/page.tsx                   SCR-VER-18  Inspections and records of the authority
    records/[record]/correct/page.tsx  SCR-VER-15  Correct a record

The console draws no queue: the rail's entries are the way in.
