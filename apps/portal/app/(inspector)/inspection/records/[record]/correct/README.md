# correct

    page.tsx   SCR-VER-15  Correct a record (`?case=`)

The version in force beside the correction: the case it is made under, with what CAS answers for it (DR-747 rows 1
to 5, said in the header with Issue unavailable), the corrected outcome and findings as at conclusion, the
follow-up and the reason. Issue asks for confirmation; DR-741 refusals come back beside the outcome or the field
they name. Issued, version n+1 opens on SCR-VER-18 and the earlier version is kept, marked superseded. A case
admits one correction under its decision in force: CORRECTION_ALREADY_RECORDED names the version issued under it, and
once an appeal varies the decision with a new correction (CAS's appeal outcome), a further version is admitted.
