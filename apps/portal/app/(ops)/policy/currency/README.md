    page.tsx   SCR-PUB-17  publication currency: each approved change to a source the public service describes, overdue
               first, with the time it took to reach the public pages; filtered by state and by the period it was
               approved in (PERIOD_INVALID beside the field). The overdue step of the sweep runs as the list is read:
               a watch past its due instant is overdue with EXC-PUB-02 recorded, and the policy owner is told
               through COM (NTF-PUB-01, `recordNoticeEvent`); COM takes no raise from this family in P1, so the
               notice reference stays empty and the raise is repeated once a sweep interval.
