# inspections

    page.tsx          SCR-VER-16  Oversight of inspections and supplies
    export/route.ts   its Export CSV: the month's counts per authority, no name

A month's supply headers (never an entry) and the inspections counted per authority by state, outcome and delivery,
with their totals. The month is the latest with anything supplied or inspected unless one is chosen; the September
2026 counts are the board's, of inspections the seed does not hold one by one, plus the world's own, so an act made
now moves them. Who requested a supply is shown to a reader holding ACT-AUD only (srs/02, GET /inspection-lists/{id}).
