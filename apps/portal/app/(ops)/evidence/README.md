# evidence

The evidence console ("Minh chứng", `components/shells/ops-nav.ts`), as the PLR evidence frames draw it: the evidence
operator's (ACT-EVD, Phan Gia Lượng) desk for what devices send and the policies that judge it.

    acceptance/            SCR-PLR-07  evidence acceptance: a device's latest batch, the console's landing
    playback-decisions/    SCR-PLR-15  playback decisions of a device's local day, and its CSV export
    offline-continuity/    SCR-PLR-13  offline-continuity policy: a draft with its four parts, and its approval
    certification/         SCR-PLR-10  the certification register, where applications are assessed
    assurance-policy/      SCR-PLR-11  the device-assurance policy's versions: draft, approve and publish
    playback-rules/        SCR-PLR-15  the loudness and transition rule set the decisions name: draft and approve

Integrity findings, quarantine and disposition and the evidence policy (SCR-PLR-08, 09, 12) are not built: their rail
entries stay out of the rail until they are.
