# [credential]

SCR-IAM-08 Device detail and revocation: the credential's state and since when, the evidence periods accepted from
the device (kept whatever happens here), and Suspend, Reinstate, Revoke, each confirmed with a reason. A reason left
out is refused FIELD_MISSING on its field; a revoked credential, a relationship that ended and a reader without the
authority are said above the device with their codes.
