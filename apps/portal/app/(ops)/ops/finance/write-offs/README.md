# write-offs

    [amount]/page.tsx   SCR-FIN-15  the write-off proposed on one outstanding amount (OUT-)

Opened from the amount's reference on SCR-FIN-14, for any amount a write-off is proposed on: the canvas's Khách sạn
Nắng Xiên, or one the officer proposed there. Segregation of duties is the backend's: the approver who
proposed a write-off reads it under the same-actor line, and an officer who is not an approver reads it waiting.
