# [device]

SCR-PLR-02 Device record: the condition in force, the earlier ones at their own dates, and Record a condition, a
form in a dialog and then its confirmation. A loss or compromise quarantines the evidence from the reported time and
IAM revokes the device's credential; a transfer decides what becomes of the evidence the device still holds.

The operations officer opens it from SCR-PLR-01; the auditor (PERM-472) from SCR-PLR-07, and reads it with Record a
condition shown unavailable; the security officer from SCR-IAM-08, and records a condition (PERM-473), named on the
record under that role, but not a transfer, which is the operations officer's alone (PERM-474). A condition dated
before the latest one is refused CONDITION_OVERLAPS whichever condition it asks for, as AC-FR-PLR-004-04 reads a
retirement dated before a suspension. A transfer asked again for the premises it went to answers as it did.
