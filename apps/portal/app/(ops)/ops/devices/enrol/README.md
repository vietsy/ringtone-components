# enrol

SCR-PLR-03 Enrol a device: its identity, its supplier (whose certification is said as soon as it is chosen) and the
premises with a Player service it will serve. The device is Newly enrolled until DR-215 finds a current certification
and a pairing with that premises; DEVICE_ALREADY_ENROLLED takes the place of the steps beside the form, naming the
device that holds the identity and the premises it is enrolled at (AC-FR-PLR-003-05), with a link to its record.
