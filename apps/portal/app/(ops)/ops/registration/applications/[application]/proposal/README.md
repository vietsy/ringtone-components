# proposal

    page.tsx    SCR-REG-13  decision proposal: the outcome, the statement, the dates

As applied for, the decision is issued at once (Issue the decision); otherwise or refused, it needs its statement
and waits for a business approver other than the officer (Send for approval). The fee on each outcome is TAR's for
the facts the case file holds; where no approved tariff answers them the fee says so, and sending is refused
TARIFF_NOT_APPROVED with nothing recorded.
