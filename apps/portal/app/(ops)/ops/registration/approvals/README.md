# approvals

    page.tsx    SCR-REG-14  approve or return a proposal: the reader's worklist and the proposal opened

The list holds the proposals waiting that the reader did not prepare, oldest first; `?proposal=` opens one, from
the list or a link. The officer who prepared it may open it and is refused SEGREGATION_REQUIRED, both controls
unavailable with the reason; a member without the approver's authority is refused NOT_AUTHORISED. Approving
issues the outcome at once: a decision the premises reads on SCR-REG-08, or a refusal with its statement.
