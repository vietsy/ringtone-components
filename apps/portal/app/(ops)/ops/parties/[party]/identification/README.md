# identification

    page.tsx   SCR-PTY-03  identify a party in DRAFT: search the source, or declare by hand

DR-200 decides what a declaration does: where the source answered with no match it goes to review and the party is
REFERRED (the page then shows the declaration and its review); otherwise it stands at once. An identified party has
nothing to identify and goes back to its page. `?view=no-source` and `?view=source-unreachable` draw the P1 frame and
an outage of the source for review; the world's source is configured and answers.
