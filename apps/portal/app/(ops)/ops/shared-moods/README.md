Content operations on the operations console. The moderator (ACT-MOD) prepares moods and versions and withdraws a
draft; operations (ACT-OPS) approve a version someone else prepared and withdraw an approved or effective one. A
version in effect is what a premises' playlist editor (SCR-VEN-07) offers from its effective date.
