# policy

    page.tsx   SCR-CAS-18  case policy register, one register open (`?register=`)

Entries are added on the code-and-label registers (pause grounds, reopening grounds) and period versions prepared
on the three period kinds; the statutory limits and the remedy requirements are read here and changed by their own
owners. A protected category's policy (CFG-50) is prepared here by the security owner (ACT-SEC): the specialist
process, the access list and two different escalation addressees (ADDRESSEES_NOT_DISTINCT). Until a category has an
approved policy, no case can be classified into it.

Every prepared item is approved and published, or refused with a reason its preparer then reads, by someone who did
not prepare it (SEG_SAME_ACTOR): a business approver, or for a category policy the data protection officer as well.
The appeal-period register names the appeals lodged while no period was approved, which an approval does not reach
back to; approving a version whose effective date would cover one of them is PERIOD_VERSION_NOT_APPROVED, naming it.
