# player-assurance-policy

SCR-PLR-11 Device-assurance policy, public: the version in effect (`?version=` reads another published one), what it
fixes, its full text, and the earlier versions still readable at their own dates. A version not published is refused
CRITERIA_VERSION_NOT_PUBLISHED and Apply for certification stays visible and unavailable.

It reads the browser's world: a version the evidence console (`/evidence/assurance-policy`) approves and publishes
appears here from that moment, and a draft or a version approved but kept unpublished is answered as not published.
