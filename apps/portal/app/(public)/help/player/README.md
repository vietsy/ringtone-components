The guide in effect, 2026.1 from 15 September 2026: four parts, enrol, pair, synchronise, recover, the first open
(and the second on a wide screen), each a numbered list; then the help routes of the journey PLAYER (the support
line and the illustrated guide) and "Trợ giúp". `?at=` reads the guide as at a day: before 15 September 2026 no
version existed (the empty state, the routes still shown); a day not yet come is INSTANT_INVALID.

`?view=` shows any view the canvas drew: populated (the default), routes-unread, empty, instant-invalid, loading,
error.
